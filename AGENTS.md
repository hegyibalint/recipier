# Recipier

## Development style

Recipier is an MVP; but this doesn't mean we should be sloppy.

On the contrary, being an MVP allows us to play and find the perfect
architecture that fits our problems well. If you feel growing pains: longer and
longer files, styles, README.md getting huge, things slapped around; stop and
co-work with the user to establish a better pattern.

What an MVP stage allows us is to focus on core functionality, and forget about
corner-cases a bit. We have a simple job, we probably have a simple solution for
it.
